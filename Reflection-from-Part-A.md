Through this activity, I learned about Agentic Software Engineering and how AI agents can help developers make software development faster and more efficient. I understood how AI agents differ from traditional applications because they can analyze information, make decisions, and adjust their actions based on specific goals. I also learned why Agile methods are useful in developing AI systems, especially through continuous testing, feedback, and teamwork. Most importantly, I realized that using AI comes with risks such as security threats, privacy issues, and incorrect decisions. This activity helped me understand that although AI can make software development easier, human guidance and supervision are still important to ensure that applications remain safe, reliable, and ethical.
